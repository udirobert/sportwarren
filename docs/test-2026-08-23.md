## 3. Sunday flow

1. **Pre-session** (kickoff minus ~30–60 min): DM the record keeper the
   single reveal link + the standard first-contact framing (bold call
   is already on each player's card). Post nothing in the group chat
   unprompted.
2. **During**: do not watch.
3. **Post-session**: hand the record keeper the results the same way
   they'd normally get them (quote-reply the chat / paste the scores /
   tell them). Test whether they reach for the app to log it, or whether
   you have to push.
4. **Sunday night**: recap + per-player analysis surfaces are live for
   the group. The commitment bundle ("same time next Sunday?") is on
   each card. Do not chase anyone who hasn't opened their card yet.
